Your privacy.

A simple explanation of how this Phase 1 website works.

No account needed.

You can explore this website without signing in. Account entry points only open an information message. There are no credential fields, and the website does not create accounts or accept passwords.

The demo stays in your browser.

The homepage demo uses fixed, fictional sample information. Your choice and its feedback are held only in the current page. Refreshing the page clears them. The demo does not contact a live AI model or upload your choice.

Your lesson practice stays on the page.

Simulation choices, quiz answers, checklist ticks and written practice are held only during the current visit to a lesson. They are not uploaded or saved. Refreshing or leaving the page clears this practice. Written answers use self-review, with no AI grading or microphone recording.

No tracking tools in this release.

The site code does not set cookies, use analytics, load advertising, or store your learning progress in local storage. Fonts and application assets are served with the site. Search runs in your browser.

As with other websites, your hosting provider may process connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs, when you load a page. The hosting provider’s own policies apply to that processing.

Optional listening and translation.

Quick Review can prepare a question and answer for Google Translate. Opening its link shares that public Q&A text with Google. Nothing is sent to Google just by reading a review or opening Smart Listener. Translation and speech playback are provided by Google, and its privacy practices apply. Our site does not record your voice.

AI Guide structure.

The Guide panel can show the curriculum outlines and the selected approved Q&A. Live AI replies are not connected in this stage. Opening the panel or choosing a card does not send your message to an online AI provider. Conversations are held in page memory for this visit and clear when the page closes or reloads.

When online replies are intentionally enabled later, sending a message will share your message, selected learning context and recent conversation with the DeTLeng Guide service and its AI provider. The panel includes a processing notice and Privacy details. Temporary request handling may be used to prevent duplicate replies.

External links.

Links to Automation Desk and other DeTLeng sites open in a new tab. Those sites may have different features and privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing information.

Later features need their own explanation.

If accounts, saved progress, analytics, or live AI tools are introduced, this page should be updated before those features are enabled. No live authentication is configured in Phase 1.

Privacy details

When online replies are enabled, sending a message shares your message, the selected lesson or Q&A, and recent messages from this conversation with the DeTLeng Guide service and its AI provider. When online replies are enabled, the service may temporarily hold request results to prevent duplicate replies; provider handling follows its own policies.
