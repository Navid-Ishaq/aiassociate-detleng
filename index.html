A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you begin.

A clear picture of what is here today—and what comes next.

Is this free to explore?+

Yes. The Phase 1 website, curriculum directory, and homepage demo are free to explore. You do not need an account.

Are all 96 lessons available?+

The complete curriculum map and the first interactive lesson, What Is an LLM?, are available. The other 95 lessons are marked Coming Soon.

Do I need to know how to code?+

No. You can begin with AI concepts and everyday examples. More technical topics are there when you want to explore them.

Is the demo connected to a real AI model?+

No. It uses fixed, fictional examples in your browser. It is a simulation designed to help you practise checking evidence. No live AI request is made.

Will I receive a qualification or a job?+

This is an independent learning initiative. It does not promise an accredited qualification, certification, job placement, or income. The aim is practical understanding you can explain and demonstrate.

What is planned for later?+

More interactive lessons will follow the first lesson. Accounts, saved progress, AI Mentor, and live AI chat are future possibilities, not active features.

Privacy details

When online replies are enabled, sending a message shares your message, the selected lesson or Q&A, and recent messages from this conversation with the DeTLeng Guide service and its AI provider. The planned provider is Groq. Opening the panel or choosing a card does not send a message to an AI provider.

Chat history stays in this page's memory for this visit. When online replies are enabled, the service may temporarily hold request results to prevent duplicate replies; provider handling follows its own policies. There is no account history or microphone recording. Do not share passwords, private customer records, or other sensitive information. Lesson pages and Quick Review remain available without AI.
