Privacy details

When online replies are enabled, sending a message shares your message, the selected lesson or Q&A, and recent messages from this conversation with the DeTLeng Guide service and its AI provider. The planned provider is Groq. Opening the panel or choosing a card does not send a message to an AI provider.

Chat history stays in this page's memory for this visit. When online replies are enabled, the service may temporarily hold request results to prevent duplicate replies; provider handling follows its own policies. There is no account history or microphone recording. Do not share passwords, private customer records, or other sensitive information. Lesson pages and Quick Review remain available without AI.
