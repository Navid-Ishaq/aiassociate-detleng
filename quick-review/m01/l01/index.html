What Is an LLM?

18 questions

QUESTION 01
Customer:

What is an LLM?

Your response:

LLM stands for Large Language Model. It is an AI model trained on large amounts of text and sometimes other data to understand and generate language.

QUESTION 02
Customer:

How does an LLM actually work?

Your response:

An LLM processes your input and uses learned patterns and the available context to predict possible next tokens—small units of text. It builds a response step by step by selecting these tokens. This can produce useful text, but it does not automatically verify that the answer is true.

QUESTION 03
Customer:

What can an LLM help me do?

Your response:

An LLM can help you write emails, summarize documents, explain concepts, translate text, and answer questions. Some models can also help with coding, analysis, and other complex tasks.

QUESTION 04
Customer:

Is an LLM the same as a search engine?

Your response:

No, a search engine finds information from indexed sources, while an LLM generates responses using learned patterns and available context. Some AI applications connect an LLM to search tools, but that depends on the application.

QUESTION 05
Customer:

Does an LLM know everything about the world?

Your response:

No, an LLM has limitations and may lack recent, specialized, or private information. Even when it knows a lot about a topic, its answers are not guaranteed to be correct.

QUESTION 06
Customer:

If an LLM gives a confident answer, does that mean the answer is correct?

Your response:

Not necessarily. An LLM can sound confident even when its answer is inaccurate, so important facts should be checked against reliable sources.

QUESTION 07
Customer:

Does an LLM understand language in the same way humans do?

Your response:

Not exactly. An LLM learns complex patterns and relationships in language, but that does not mean it has human experiences or understands the world in the same way people do.

QUESTION 08
Customer:

Can an LLM think and reason through difficult problems?

Your response:

Some LLMs can solve complex problems and perform useful reasoning tasks. However, they can make logical mistakes, so their reasoning and conclusions should be checked when accuracy matters.

QUESTION 09
Customer:

Where does an LLM get the information it uses in an answer?

Your response:

An LLM uses patterns learned during training and information provided in the current conversation. Depending on the application and its permissions, it may also use connected documents or external tools.

QUESTION 10
Customer:

If I ask the AI the same factual question twice and get the same answer, is it verified?

Your response:

No. Repeating an answer does not provide independent evidence that it is correct. Check the claim against a reliable source that actually supports it.

QUESTION 11
Customer:

Can an LLM give me information about something that happened today?

Your response:

It may not know about recent events unless current information is available through a connected tool or another source. For today's information, check whether the application can access up-to-date sources.

QUESTION 12
Customer:

Why did the LLM give me an answer that sounds correct but contains false information?

Your response:

An LLM sometimes generates incorrect or unsupported information, which is often called a hallucination. This happens because producing a fluent answer is not the same as verifying that the answer is true.

QUESTION 13
Customer:

Can I trust an LLM to calculate financial figures or solve mathematical problems?

Your response:

An LLM can help explain calculations and work through mathematical problems, but it can make mistakes. For important figures, verify the results with a calculator, spreadsheet, or reliable calculation tool.

QUESTION 14
Customer:

Can I use an LLM to understand a legal or medical document?

Your response:

Yes, an LLM can help explain difficult terms and summarize information in simpler language. However, it can misunderstand important details and should not replace a qualified professional when expert judgment is needed.

QUESTION 15
Customer:

Does every AI-generated response need an internet search?

Your response:

No. Rewriting supplied text or creating a fictional greeting may only need a review of its wording and meaning. Claims about real orders, payments, dates, or policies need appropriate evidence.

QUESTION 16
Customer:

How can I get a more useful answer from an LLM?

Your response:

Explain what you need, provide relevant context, and describe the format you want. For example, instead of asking "Explain AI," ask "Explain AI to a beginner using three simple examples."

QUESTION 17
Customer:

If I want to use an LLM for customer support, what should I be careful about?

Your response:

An LLM can help draft replies and explain common issues, but it may provide incorrect information or misunderstand a customer's situation. Use reliable reference material, respect data permissions, and verify important answers before sharing them.

QUESTION 18
Customer:

What is the most important thing I should remember when using an LLM?

Your response:

An LLM is a powerful language tool, but a convincing answer is not necessarily a verified fact. Use it to support your work, check important information, and apply human judgment when making decisions.
