## Phase 1 decisions

- One working interactive learning demo is included as explicitly required by the current build brief, even though the blueprint's broader roadmap places richer exercises later.
- Every individual lesson is Coming Soon. No authored lesson, quiz bank, certificate, user progress, AI Mentor, or live model service is invented.
- The fictional workshop is a teaching example, not a real event. Demo outputs are fixed examples, not live AI responses or measured model behavior.
- Credentials are never accepted. Account wording exactly follows the user's addendum.
- Required ecosystem links retain their URLs, secure new-tab behavior, and fixed footer order.
- No external provider affiliation, accredited qualification, guaranteed employment, or earnings claims are made.
